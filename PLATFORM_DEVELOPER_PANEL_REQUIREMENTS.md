# Platform Developer Panel Requirements

Fawtara is the platform owner. Tenant companies such as cake and chocolate businesses must be managed separately from platform ownership.

## Required platform owner tools

- Create tenant company
- Activate or suspend tenant company
- Set subscription plan
- Set trial period and expiry date
- Set max users and invoice limits
- View tenant health status
- View tenant usage summary
- Export tenant data
- Restore tenant data
- View audit logs

## Required tenant company settings

- Company profile
- Branch profile
- Departments
- Employee roles
- Invoice rules
- Product categories
- Discount rules
- Delivery settings
- Reports permissions

## Demo access policy

Demo access must be isolated in its own tenant company and must not grant platform owner permissions.

Target demo market:

- Cake companies
- Chocolate companies
- Sweets and hospitality companies
